## 3. Roles and responsibilities

**School**
- Assign facilitators; complete PD log; follow site privacy / COPPA / FERPA practices
- Obtain any required parent/guardian consents for under-13 accounts
- Conduct observations and keep student work samples with appropriate consent
- Fill [pilot narrative](cognia-stem-evidence.md) and local accreditation binders
- Do not claim Kanam confers school accreditation or that AP CSP Prep is an official College Board course

**Provider**
- Provide licensed platform access for agreed seats / term
- Maintain curriculum docs, facilitator guides, and standards crosswalks
- Support onboarding and escalate product issues through agreed channels
- Not act as the School’s Cognia accreditation applicant

## 4. Data, privacy, and student information

- School remains controller of student education records under its policies
- Provider processes account and progress data per its privacy policy and applicable law
- No sale of student personal information; use limited to providing and improving the service
- Parent/guardian access and DSAR paths: product Parent hub + School procedures ([parent communication kit](parent-communication-kit.md))
- Screenshots / demos for marketing require separate written consent

## 5. Intellectual property

Curriculum content, platform software, and trademarks remain Provider property. Student-created work remains with the student/School under School policy. School may print facilitator guides and rubrics for classroom use during the term.

## 6. Observation and accreditation evidence

School may use Provider materials (session guides, rubrics, PD log, standards maps) as STEM evidence. Provider may request anonymized aggregate completion metrics for product improvement; School may decline sharing identifiable student data.

## 9. Non-claims and disclaimers

School will not represent that:
1. Kanam alone confers Cognia school accreditation  
2. AP CSP Prep is an official College Board AP course  
3. Provider guarantees any student outcome or exam score  

Provider disclaims warranties not expressly stated in a signed commercial agreement.

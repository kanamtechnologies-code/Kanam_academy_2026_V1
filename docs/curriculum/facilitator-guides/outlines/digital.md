| 5 | Creation, Accessibility & IP | Accessible artifacts; IP tradeoffs |
| 6 | Privacy, Security & Tradeoffs | Threats, controls, automated collection |
| 7 | Agency: Wellbeing & Troubleshooting | Personal impacts; reusable troubleshooting |
| 8 | Futures & Capstone | Careers + evaluate impacts & act |

## Facilitation tips

- Scenario debates beat lecture; require a justified recommendation, not a slogan.
- Capstone should produce a personal action plan students can share with a guardian or advisor.

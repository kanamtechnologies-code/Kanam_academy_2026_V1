# Facilitator outline — Digital Literacy

**Guide status:** outline only — **full guides:** [../digital/](../digital/)

## Week goals

| Week | Theme | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hardware, Software & Your Files | Hardware vs software, then where files live |
| 2 | Getting Online and Checking Sources | How a search travels; check a claim before you share |
| 3 | Messages and Teamwork | Messages that get answered; one shared file |
| 4 | Reputation and Who Gets Hurt | What you leave behind; who gets hurt and who helps |
| 5 | Making Things and Giving Credit | Make it usable; who owns the work |
| 6 | Scams and What Apps Take | Fake messages and bad files; what apps collect |
| 7 | How You Feel and How You Fix Things | How screens treat you; a fix someone else can follow |
| 8 | Work Ahead and Your Plan | Jobs and tools; your plan from here |

## Facilitation tips

- Scenario debates beat lecture; require a justified recommendation, not a slogan.
- Capstone should produce a personal action plan students can share with a guardian or advisor.

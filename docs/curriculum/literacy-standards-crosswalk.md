# Literacy tracks — standards crosswalk (expanded lessons)

Kanam Academy literacy lessons target **18–25 interactive slides** by topic weight (early lessons shorter, advanced/capstone lessons longer), plus knowledge checks and practice. Students do not see standards citations in the slide decks — this crosswalk is for instructors and curriculum mapping only.

## Financial Literacy (`fl-1`…`fl-16`)

Aligned to **National Standards for Personal Financial Education (Jump$tart / CEE, 2021)** — Topics I–VI, primarily grade 8–12 outcomes.

| Lesson | Focus | Jump$tart topic(s) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fl-1 | Money, goals, SMART-ish planning | Spending + Saving (decision-making) |
| fl-2 | Needs, wants, tradeoffs, opportunity cost | Spending |
| fl-3 | Paychecks, gross/net, deductions | Earning Income |
| fl-4 | Banks, accounts, debit vs credit, FDIC | Saving |
| fl-5 | Budgets (categories, 50/30/20, zero-based) | Spending |
| fl-6 | Cash flow, sinking funds, emergencies | Saving |
| fl-7 | Credit reports & scores | Managing Credit |
| fl-8 | Interest, APR, debt traps | Managing Credit |
| fl-9 | Interest & compound growth | Saving (+ Investing bridge) |
| fl-10 | Investing basics, risk, diversification | Investing |
| fl-11 | Insurance & risk transfer | Managing Risk |
| fl-12 | Scams, fraud, consumer rights | Managing Risk |
| fl-13 | Taxes & first job | Earning Income |
| fl-14 | College costs, aid, borrowing | Earning Income + Managing Credit |
| fl-15 | Big money decisions framework | Cross-topic application |
| fl-16 | Capstone personal money plan | Topics I–VI integrated |

## AI Literacy (`ai-1`…`ai-16`)

- **CSTA 2017:** Impacts of Computing (2-IC-20, 2-IC-21; 3A-IC-25/29 where relevant); Data & Analysis bridges (2-DA-07/08)
- **ISTE 2016:** 1.3 Knowledge Constructor, 1.5 Computational Thinker, 1.2 Digital Citizen (ethics/privacy lessons)

## Cybersecurity (`cs-1`…`cs-16`)

- Defensive literacy aligned to **NIST NICE** awareness themes (Identify / Protect / Detect) — recognition and response, not exploitation
- **CSTA:** Networks & Impacts concepts at Level 2 / 3A awareness depth
- **ISTE:** Digital Citizen + Computational Thinker

## Digital Citizenship (`dl-1`…`dl-16`)

- **ISTE Standards for Students (2016)** — Digital Citizen, Knowledge Constructor, Creative Communicator, Computational Thinker
- Complements CSTA Impacts of Computing (privacy, ethics, collaboration)

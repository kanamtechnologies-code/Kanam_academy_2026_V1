# Kanam Academy · Move forward.

**Browser-based CS, AI literacy, and data skills for grades 6–10**  
*Built for classrooms, enrichment programs, and families — live, hybrid, or self-paced.*

---

### The problem
Students need credible pathways into **coding, AI, data, and digital citizenship** — not more unstructured screen time. Schools need programs that are **standards-aligned, measurable, and manageable** without requiring a full-time CS teacher.

### The solution
**Kanam Academy** is a web platform where learners in grades **6–10** follow structured 8-week programs, run **real Python and SQL in the browser**, and earn **XP and badges** as they master each skill. Every lesson **explains, checks, and corrects itself** — so students progress confidently and adults can support with minimal prep.

---

### Four programs · one platform

| Program | Lessons | What students gain |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **AI Literacy** — understand & use AI wisely | 16 | Prompting, verification, bias, privacy, academic integrity |
| **Digital Literacy** — safe, smart tech for school & work | 16 | Search, citizenship, security, content creation, career readiness |
| **AI + Python** — build your first AI helper | 13 | Variables → logic → loops → functions → **AI NPC capstone** |
| **Data Analyst** — SQL, tables & charts | 14 | Query real data → summarize → **visualize & present findings** |

*59 interactive lessons · 8-week pacing (2 sessions/week) · capstone in every track*

---

### How it works
**Learn it** (short lesson module) → **Do the activity** (4 auto-graded exercises, instant feedback) → **Reflect** (ethics + check-for-understanding) → **Earn** XP & badges on a visible roadmap. **No software install** — works on Chromebooks and laptops.

### Why schools & partners choose Kanam
- **Standards-aligned** — CSTA (2017 L2/L3A), K–12 CS Framework, ISTE, Common Core Math (data strand); accreditation documentation included  
- **Evidence of mastery** — auto-graded code/SQL, capstones, per-student progress reporting  
- **Flexible delivery** — live cohort, hybrid, self-paced enrichment, or homeschool  
- **Low staff burden** — coach’s notes, plain-language explainers, instructor dashboard  
- **Pilot-ready** — demo/guest mode for families and decision-makers before purchase

The challenge

Students need credible pathways into coding, AI, data, and digital citizenship — not unstructured screen time. Schools need programs that are measurable and manageable without a dedicated CS teacher.

The answer

Kanam Academy delivers 59 interactive lessons across four tracks. Learners run real code, get instant feedback, and progress on a visible roadmap — no software to install.
